# The Sixth Awakening — Character Roster

Contains full story spoilers. Working design reference based on PROJECT_DESCRIPTION.md and the story photo 3246a7a7-9331-4812-a760-06fcfa67e79c.jpeg. The checklist is in f21bff49-3377-4ebc-9225-b6f8597d1bc0.jpeg.

## Status and scope

The protagonists, named bosses, six-level sequence, and ending below come from the supplied story photo. Regular enemy names, combat behaviors, visual directions, and supporting NPC roles are proposals, not previously established facts. Here, bots means AI-controlled enemies and NPCs, not robots.

The short description calls the bosses six lieutenants. The detailed photo makes the sixth boss a towering dream version of Veyr. For this draft, the Dream Sovereign occupies the sixth boss slot; real Veyr is the final phase of Level 6, not a seventh level. Whether the Dream Sovereign is an independent lieutenant impersonating Veyr or a direct projection remains unresolved.

## Main cast — established characters

| Character | Story and gameplay role | Proposed design requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kael | Former guardian driven by guilt. Main playable character through Levels 1–5 and the dream portion of Level 6. Believes Mira died and pursues revenge while still chained in the real throne room. Wakes near the ending to help sever the final chain. | Readable guardian silhouette and sword; damaged armor; wrist-chain motif. Requires playable, real-world captive, and weakened ending states. Proposed combat: deliberate sword attacks, movement, dodging, and parries. |
| Mira | Equally capable partner whose apparent death motivates Kael. Appears fighting in the opening, through fragmented memories, and alive in the reveal. Becomes playable in the final phase: shields Kael, breaks the dream engine, and fights Veyr. Survives with a lasting injury. | Distinct silhouette from Kael; lighter equipment suited to fast counter-based combat. Requires opening, injured playable, and ending states. Injury must remain consistent through the ending. |
| Veyr — real form | Demon sovereign who imprisons Kael and feeds him false victories. Present in the opening and the true final confrontation against Mira. | Controlled, imposing throne-room presence, visually related to the Dream Sovereign. Proposed attacks combine direct strikes and dream-engine pulses, giving Mira counterattack opportunities. |

## Six dream bosses — established identities and level roles

Combat behaviors and visual directions below are proposals. The photo lists denial, rage, bargaining, despair, memory, and truth as collective themes without assigning each theme to a specific boss; that mapping remains open.

| Level / boss | Established purpose | Proposed combat behavior and design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — The Iron Gaoler | Warden of the shifting-chain prison; claims Mira died. Kael defeats him to escape. | Heavy armored jailer with chain weapon and keys. Slow telegraphed swings teach parrying; chain sweeps teach jumping; missed heavy attacks create punish windows. |
| 2 — The Red Huntress | Pursued across burning rooftops as the soldier believed to have delivered the killing blow. | Lean hunter with a red visual accent and long blade. Pursuit followed by a duel: lunges, retreating attacks, and rooftop repositioning test movement and timing. Her guilt is presented through Kael's unreliable perception. |
| 3 — The Collector | Rules the hall of stolen voices, where a recording of Mira contradicts Kael's memory. | Robed figure carrying vessels for stolen voices. Uses delayed sound attacks and decoys; consistent visual tells keep the fight readable without audio. |
| 4 — The Mercy Priest | Presides over a sanctuary offering false reunions. Kael must refuse an illusion of Mira. | Ceremonial robes and a broken halo. Creates deceptive safe areas, binding attacks, and healing illusions. Exposed recovery windows reward recognizing the deception. |
| 5 — The Mirror Knight | Apparent copy of Kael. Flashes of the real throne room interrupt the battle. | Distorted guardian armor and mirrored sword. Echoes a limited set of Kael's moves with readable delays; rewards feints and varied timing rather than reading inputs unfairly. |
| 6 — The Dream Sovereign | Towering dream version of Veyr. The arena tears apart as Kael lands the apparent finishing strike, revealing his captivity. | Exaggerated Veyr silhouette, inverted throne imagery, fractured arena. Combines familiar attacks from prior encounters. Defeat triggers the reveal and switch to Mira. |

## Regular enemies / combat bots — proposed roster

These are reusable enemy types, not individually named story characters. Start with two per level; difficulty variants should reuse the same base designs where possible.

| Location | Enemy type | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1: shifting prison | Chain Guard | Patrols corridors and uses clear single melee strikes, teaching basic attacks and parries. |
| Level 1: shifting prison | Shackled Wretch | Crawls or rushes toward Kael, then pauses after a committed lunge; teaches spacing and crowd awareness. |
| Level 2: burning rooftops | Ember Stalker | Fast melee pursuer that jumps between platforms and pressures movement. |
| Level 2: burning rooftops | Ash Archer | Fires visibly telegraphed projectiles from rooftop positions; teaches approaching ranged enemies. |
| Level 3: hall of stolen voices | Whisper Husk | Projects misleading sounds and advances in short bursts; its actual attack always has a visible cue. |
| Level 3: hall of stolen voices | Voice Keeper | Stationary ranged controller that sends timed sound pulses across lanes; pressures the player to close distance. |
| Level 4: corrupted sanctuary | Hollow Acolyte | Supports nearby enemies with a visible protective tether; creates a target-priority choice. |
| Level 4: corrupted sanctuary | Penitent Sentinel | Slow shield bearer with a punishable recovery after a heavy strike; tests patience and positioning. |
| Level 5: mirror arena | Mirror Shade | Replays a short, telegraphed sequence of Kael-like attacks; prepares the player for the Mirror Knight. |
| Level 5: mirror arena | Fractured Guardian | Damaged echo of a guardian whose stance visibly signals its next attack pattern. |
| Level 6: dream arena | Dream Remnant | Reuses distorted versions of earlier enemy patterns during the approach to the Dream Sovereign. Avoid crowding the main boss duel. |
| Level 6: real throne room / opening | Throne Guard | Real elite soldier fought by Mira in the opening and finale. Uses disciplined strikes and leaves counter windows; briefly threatens captive Kael. |

## Initial art inventory

- 3 core character designs: Kael, Mira, real Veyr.
- 6 dream boss designs, including the Dream Sovereign.
- 12 regular enemy types; Dream Remnants reuse earlier enemies, and other shared rigs or parts can reduce work.
- 1 optional Prisoner Echo design.
- Reused apparition assets and story-state variants for Mira, Kael, and background figures.

This is 21 primary character/enemy design entries, plus 1 optional NPC base. It is not 22 unique narrative people or necessarily 22 independent sprite rigs. Mirror Knight, Dream Sovereign, and Dream Remnants should share visual ancestry with existing designs while retaining readable silhouettes.

## Suggested design order

1. Kael and Mira together: establish proportions, art style, weapons, and readable silhouettes.
2. Real Veyr and Dream Sovereign together: establish their relationship and contrast.
3. Iron Gaoler, Chain Guard, and Shackled Wretch: complete the first level's cast as a production sample.
4. Remaining bosses and each level's two regular enemy types.
5. Story variants, apparitions, and optional supporting NPCs.

Before final sprite production, settle art style, character scale, equipment, and the sixth-boss identity. The specific injury Mira carries into the ending is also still undesigned.
