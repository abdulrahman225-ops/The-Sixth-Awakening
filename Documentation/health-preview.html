The Sixth Awakening · Health states

Editable UI designs for Kael, Mira, ordinary enemies, and bosses. Damage and healing update the percentage and state label. Healing cannot revive a defeated actor; Reset starts a new life. Values are provisional.

Healthy: above 50% · Wounded: 26–50% · Critical: 1–25% · Dead: 0%. The percentage thresholds apply continuously. This preview demonstrates UI states, not combat or invulnerability timing.
