Identity reference only: remove all reference-sheet text, portraits, backdrop and detail insets. EXACT 4 columns by 6 rows = 24 distinct full-body animation poses. Portrait canvas, preferably 1024x1536. Every pose must be completely ISOLATED from all others by generous EMPTY transparent space. Treat canvas as 4x6 equal squares; fit entire character AND weapon into the middle 65 percent of each cell; no element extends into neighbor cells. Body scale consistent across ALL frames. Face right, side-scroller orthographic side view, feet aligned for ground actions. Simplified hand-painted 2D sprites, strong outlines and readable shapes; simplify tiny details, consistent clothes and equipment. All poses entirely in their own cells, especially horizontal death and weapon extension. Keep weapons compact to fit. No gore. IMPORTANT transparent separation at least 30 pixels between ANY two silhouettes. A practical sprite atlas, not a concept illustration.
