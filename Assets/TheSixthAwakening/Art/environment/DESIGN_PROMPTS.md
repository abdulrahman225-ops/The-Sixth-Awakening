# Animated environmental props

Proposed initial asset set: 4 animated props per setting, 7 settings across 6 levels (Level 6 dream and real variants), 28 props and 112 animation frames. Generated using built-in image generation with the corresponding background as style reference. Props are separate from backgrounds and gameplay collision/interaction code.
