# Level background design prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Seven opaque environment paintings for six levels: Level 6 has a dream scene and the real throne-room finale. Intended as flat background plates behind separate gameplay platforms; these are not seamless tiles, layered parallax exports, or collision geometry. Visual environment details are proposed designs based on the established story.
